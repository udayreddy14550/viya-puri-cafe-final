    About us

    A neighbourhood café in Chowdavaram

    Viya Puri Café is on the road beside SBI Bank in Lalitha Pitam. We serve pani puri and chaat, burgers, mocktails, desserts, thick shakes, lassi, tea and coffee, and you can order online for home delivery.

    Our motto is simple: Good Food. Good Mood.
